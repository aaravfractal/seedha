LABELLING RULE
Keep rule 11: the sample hotel's data stays fictional and labelled, but
quietly: one "Sample hotel" badge in the hotel chip plus the existing
footer. Remove repeated per-item "Demo" or "Sample" pills inside the new
inbox threads and room board, except where a figure is illustrative
(keep "Illustration" there). Never claim real customers.

PRIORITY
MUST: A (room board) and B (inbox and invoices). SHOULD: C (Pro page).

A. "LAST NIGHT" ROOM BOARD (owner Overview)
1. Each room tile is a button. Tap or click opens a side sheet: room,
   channel, nights, list rate, discount, commission, GST on commission,
   fees, net kept (all from the API ledger, never computed in the
   browser), guest initials only, consent status (Consented at Desk or
   not), and actions: "Open in Desk", "Message guest" (only if
   consented), "Print slip".
2. "If this room had been direct": shows how much more the hotel would
   have kept, computed by the API with the ruling 5 formula, labelled
   "Illustration".
3. Channel legend chips filter and highlight rooms (others dim); counts
   update.
4. Night scrubber: last 7 nights (sample closes from seed data), with the
   summary line (rooms sold, net, direct share) animating on change.
5. Motion: tiles enter with a 30ms stagger; filter changes cross-fade;
   sheet slides in 450ms with the app curve; reduced motion makes it
   instant. Keyboard: arrow keys move between rooms, Enter opens, Escape
   closes. Phone: tiles 4 per row, sheet becomes a bottom sheet.
6. New API: /rooms/nights (last 7 night summaries) and /rooms/{night}/
   {room} detail, owner-only, tests included.

B. GUEST INBOX WITH REALISTIC THREADS AND AI INVOICES
1. Seed 8 consented guests for the sample hotel (each with a Desk
   consent record so rule 4 holds) with realistic WhatsApp threads in
   English, Hindi and Hinglish: invoice request for a past stay; late
   checkout request; early check-in; parking for two cars; Diwali
   booking enquiry; Wi-Fi not working in room 204; refund question for a
   cancelled night; directions from Dehradun with luggage. Mix of read,
   unread and replied states. Seed re-creates them on "Reset demo data".
2. "Simulate incoming message" control (owner Settings and the inbox
   overflow menu) that adds one new realistic guest message from a
   rotating set, so the inbox is never empty in a demo.
3. AI drafts for every new message (Claude when the key is set, fixed
   templates otherwise). Drafts appear as suggestions; the owner edits
   and taps Send. Nothing is sent automatically (rule 7). Messages are
   simulated sends unless WhatsApp keys exist.
4. Invoice on request: when a guest asks for an invoice or bill, the
   draft attaches a "Stay invoice" PDF generated by code from the booking
   and payment rows: hotel name and address, GSTIN from Settings (hidden
   if empty), invoice number, guest name, stay dates, room, nights, rate,
   discounts, extras, GST at the rate in Settings, total paid, payment
   method, and "Thank you for staying with us". The AI writes only the
   covering message; every amount comes from code. Footer: "Generated by
   Seedha. Confirm the tax invoice format with your accountant."
   The owner can preview, download and attach. Tests: totals equal the
   booking row; no AI text contains a rupee figure (guard D6).
5. Profile column: consent date, stays, last invoice, notes, "What Seedha
   remembers" (preferences only).

C. INTERACTIVE SEEDHA PRO PAGE
Each card expands in place (shared-layout animation) into a live mini
view from the API: Audit (flagged rows and the Seedha Recover dispute
draft); Pulse (mini season timeline with channel decisions); Rate Watch
(sample screenshot comparison with the parity gap); Tonight Card (rooms
left and best channel tonight); Who Paid the Discount (split bar per OTA
promotion); Floor by Channel (slider showing the lowest rate worth taking
per channel after commission and GST); Group Displacement (calculator:
group size and rate vs rooms pushed out); Agent Khata (agent ledger
table); Two-Property Rollup (property toggle); Seedha Proof (link to
Proof). Keep "Pro" pills and "Sample" where data is sample. Escape and a
close button collapse the card; focus returns to it.
